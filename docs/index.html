Book My Desk

Once: drag the button below onto your bookmarks bar.
Every ~3 months: log into Appspace, click the bookmark, pick your desk and days, done.

Step 1 — Save the bookmark once

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

Book My Desk

Don't see the bookmarks bar? Show it with ⌘+Shift+B (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows).

Can't drag? Add it manually instead
  1. Right-click the bookmarks bar and choose Add page… (or Add bookmark).
  2. Set the name to Book My Desk.
  3. Right-click the Book My Desk button above, then Copy Link Address, and paste it into the URL field.
  4. Save.

Step 2 — Use it

  1. Log into Appspace ↗.
  2. Click Book My Desk in the bookmarks bar.
  3. First time: pick your desk (search by name), pick your weekdays, set booking horizon.
  4. Returning: see your dashboard — click Book New Days when new days are available.
How it works

The bookmark runs a small script on the Appspace page that reads your login session (no passwords are stored or transmitted). It books desk reservations up to a year in advance, all within your browser. Preferences are saved in your browser's local storage.

Bookmark not working?

Make sure you're logged into Appspace in the same tab before clicking the bookmark. If you see a Content Security Policy error, try this: open the browser console (F12) and paste the bookmarklet code manually.
